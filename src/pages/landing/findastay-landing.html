The manifesto

A New Era in
Accommodation Pricing

For twenty years the price of a room has been something done to you. That era is ending.

The problem

Everyone negotiates. You just weren't invited.

A hotel room is not a tin of beans. Its value changes by the hour — a rainy Tuesday in winter is worth a fraction of a Saturday in summer, and owners know it.

So they discount constantly: to corporate contracts, to the big booking sites, to wholesalers, to the walk-in who asks at 8pm. The one person never offered the real price is the traveller booking online weeks ahead. You see one fixed number — and sitting inside it is a 15–18% commission the property pays the booking site and quietly builds back into your rate.

The discount already exists. We just hand you the keys to it.
The idea

Flip the transaction.

Instead of a property shouting one price into the void, the traveller opens the conversation: here's what I'll pay for these nights. The property looks at its real occupancy and answers in seconds — yes, here's my counter, or not this time.

It's how markets have always worked everywhere except the one screen you book a holiday on. No auctions, no gamesmanship, no waiting. One offer, one answer.

And that "middleman"? It's not local.

Booking.com and Expedia are offshore giants. Their commission doesn't just disappear from the price — it leaves the country entirely. Every dollar that goes to them is a dollar that never reaches the family that hosted you, never lands in the town you stayed in, and never circulates back home.

findastay keeps the whole thing between two people: you and the local who hosts you. The money you save stays in your pocket. The money they earn stays in their community. Nothing gets siphoned overseas. That's the real point — not just a cheaper night, but a fairer one.

Why now

The tech finally caught up.

Handling thousands of individual offers used to be impossible — no front desk could keep up. AI changes that. An assistant prices a fair offer, drafts it politely, and lets an owner clear a night's offers in the time it takes to make a coffee.

That's the unlock: negotiation at the scale of the whole internet, without the friction that made it unworkable before.

Fixed pricing was a limit of the old tools. It was never the fair outcome.
Questions, answered

Everything you're wondering.

New way to book, so new questions. Straight talk for travellers and owners.

For travellers

How is this different from Booking.com or Airbnb?
There you accept a fixed price with commission built in. Here you make the offer — your price, your dates — and the property accepts, counters once, or declines. You deal with the owner directly, not a call centre.
Is making an offer free?
Browsing and making offers cost nothing. If an offer is accepted and you confirm, you pay a small flat $9.99 commitment fee to lock it in — the rest of your payment goes to the property for the stay.
What if they say no?
Then that's that — no obligation, no fee. Offer on another property, or come back with a different number. Most owners counter rather than flat-out decline if you're close.
How do I know a fair price to offer?
The AI concierge suggests a sensible figure from the dates, the property and typical rates — so you're not lowballing or overpaying. Adjust it before it sends.
How do I pay?
Your choice: securely online when the offer's accepted, or pay at the property on arrival where the owner allows it. Your contact details stay private until a booking is confirmed.

For properties

What does it cost to list?
Listing is free and there's no commission or percentage of your rate — just a small commitment fee per confirmed booking, taken from the booking total: $9.99 under $500, $14.99 up to $1,000, $24.99 over $1,000. Travellers pay this when they confirm; there's never a separate bill.
Do I have to accept low offers?
Never. You set your floor. Every offer hits your dashboard and you accept, counter once, or decline. You're always in control of rates and availability.
Won't this undercut my normal rates?
It fills nights you'd otherwise leave empty. A room sold at a fair offer beats a room sold at nothing — and with no 17% commission you often net more than a full-price booking-site sale.
How long does setup take?
Minutes. Guided onboarding collects your business details, NZBN or ABN, and payout preference. If your property's already in our directory, claim it in a tap.
Can I still use other channels?
Yes. findastay is one more channel for filling rooms — run it alongside whatever you use now. Keep an eye on your own availability so you don't double-book while we build out calendar sync.
